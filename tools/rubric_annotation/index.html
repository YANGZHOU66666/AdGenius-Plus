        Rule: factual_support (v0.2 draft)
        
    
          
  • Judge only whether the ad conflicts with the product information, including the title. If the two cannot both be true, it is a conflict: False.
    • 
          
  • Typical conflicts: a different number, unit or count; a property of one part, model, package or the wider product line given to this product; a material, condition, colour or type the information rules out; a scope wider than a stated limit.
    • 
          
  • Not conflicts: leaving information out; paraphrase, summary or shorthand; anything the information neither states nor rules out, including more specific but consistent wording; subjective praise and suitable uses.
    • 
          
  • If the information contradicts itself, the title decides: matching the title is not a conflict; conflicting with it is. Ignore a title that clearly names a different product.
    • 
          
  • If the title does not settle it, conflicting with any part counts, except a stray product-details field or an obvious typo.
    • 
          
  • When the title, features or description give a number, a product-details number (item weight, product dimensions) does not override it.
    • 
          
  • For False, write the ad sentence and the conflicting information text in the reason.
